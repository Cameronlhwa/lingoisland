"你是什么星座？" (Nǐ shì shénme xīngzuò?) — "What's your star sign?"

This question comes up in Chinese conversation more than you might expect. Astrology — both Western horoscopes (星座, xīngzuò) and the Chinese zodiac (生肖, shēngxiào) — is genuinely popular in Chinese-speaking culture, especially among younger generations.

If you want the lunar-year animal vocabulary too, read [Chinese Zodiac Signs: What's Yours and How to Talk About It](/blog/zodiac-signs-in-chinese).

Knowing this vocabulary doesn't just let you answer the question. It opens up a whole category of casual, fun conversation that's perfect for intermediate learners.

### How This Comes Up in Real Conversation

Horoscope talk is a very natural icebreaker in Chinese. You might hear:

"你是什么星座？" — What's your sign?

"双子座的人很聪明。" — Gemini people are very smart.

"最近运势怎么样？" — How's your fortune been lately?

It's light, fun, and opens up a lot of natural follow-up conversation. Perfect territory for intermediate learners who want easy, low-stakes conversation practice.
