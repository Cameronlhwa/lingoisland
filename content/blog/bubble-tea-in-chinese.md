Let's be real: one of the best arguments for learning Mandarin is walking into a bubble tea shop in Taipei or Shanghai and ordering entirely in Chinese like it's nothing.

It's a small moment, but it hits different.

Bubble tea (珍珠奶茶, zhēnzhū nǎichá, literally "pearl milk tea") has its own little vocabulary universe — drink types, toppings, sweetness levels, ice levels. Once you know it, you'll be navigating any boba menu with total confidence.

### Sweetness and Ice

This is where you get to actually customise your order and sound like you know what you're doing.

| Sweetness level | Chinese | Pinyin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full sweet | 全糖 | quán táng |
| Less sweet | 少糖 | shǎo táng |
| Half sweet | 半糖 | bàn táng |
| Minimal sweet | 微糖 | wēi táng |
| No sugar | 無糖 / 无糖 | wú táng |

| Ice level | Chinese | Pinyin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal ice | 正常冰 | zhèngcháng bīng |
| Less ice | 少冰 | shǎo bīng |
| No ice | 去冰 | qù bīng |
| Warm | 温热 | wēnrè |

### A Sample Order

"一杯珍珠奶茶，半糖，少冰，谢谢！"

*Yī bēi zhēnzhū nǎichá, bàn táng, shǎo bīng, xièxiè!*

"One bubble tea, half sugar, less ice, thanks!"

Try saying that at your next boba run.

### Ordering Phrases

| Phrase | Pinyin | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 我要一杯... | wǒ yào yī bēi... | I'd like one cup of... |
| 可以换成...吗？ | kěyǐ huànchéng... ma? | Can I change it to...? |
| 不要加糖 | bù yào jiā táng | No added sugar please |
| 外带 | wài dài | To go / takeaway |
| 内用 | nèi yòng | Dine in |
